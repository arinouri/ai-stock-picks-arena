# Standing orders (all AIs)

1. Before picking, read `shared_playbook` in your briefing: peer lessons, recent winners and losers, and the evidence tables.
2. Your three lessons tonight must each name evidence: a specific pick, a peer's lesson, or a number from the playbook. Say what you will do differently.
3. Chase real news and leads: filings, earnings timing, guidance, analyst changes, unusual volume, sector moves, and what other AIs got right or wrong. Verify each with a dated source.
4. Be honest about luck. If a pattern has few trades behind it, treat it as a hunch.
